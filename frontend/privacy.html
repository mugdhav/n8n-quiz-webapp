Privacy notice and rules

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Who runs this quiz

This quiz is run by Mugdha Vairagade, n8n Ambassador, as a community activity. Contact: contact@vmugdha.in OR https://www.linkedin.com/in/mugdhav/.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your answers, whether each answer was correct, and how long you took on each question.
  • The time you started and finished.

We don't use cookies or trackers. Your browser stores a quiz session ID so you can continue after a page reload.

Why we collect it

  • To make sure each email is used to take the quiz only once.
  • To score the quiz.
  • To choose the winners and contact them by email.

We won't sell your data or add you to a mailing list.

Where it is stored

Answers are stored in a private Google Sheet owned by the organiser, through Google Apps Script. Only the organiser can access it.

How long we keep it

We delete names, emails and answers 30 days after the quiz closes.

Your choices

You can ask us to see or delete your data at any time by contacting us at the address above.

Quiz rules

  • The quiz runs over two days, from 22 September 2026, 00:00 AM UTC+5.30 to 23 September 2026, 23:59 PM UTC+5.30. Anyone with the link can take part.
  • One attempt per email address. Using several email addresses to get extra attempts disqualifies you.
  • You get 10 questions drawn at random at mixed difficulty. Each correct answer is worth 1 point. A question that times out scores 0.
  • The last question is a bonus question that you answer in your own words. Answering it earns a ★.
  • Every question has a timer. The quiz moves on when you answer or when time runs out.
  • You see your score, and your ★ if you earned one, as soon as you finish.
  • Winners are announced on 25 September 2026, 18:00 PM UTC+5.30.
